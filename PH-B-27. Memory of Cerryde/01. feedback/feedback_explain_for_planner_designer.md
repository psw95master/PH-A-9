Perry works as a **planner (기획자) and UX/UI designer**, not a developer. He said so directly on 2026-07-28 after a run of explanations pitched at an engineer.

**Why:** He runs a genuinely complex setup — two Macs, launchd agents, Tailscale, tmux, hooks — and gives sharp instructions about it, which makes it easy to mistake him for an engineer and answer in shorthand. He is fully capable of deciding; he just shouldn't have to decode `kTCCServiceFileProviderDomain|...|2` or `auth_value` to do it.

**How to apply:**
- Lead with **what it means and what to decide**, then the evidence. Not raw output first.
- Keep exact commands, paths, and IPs verbatim — precision still matters, and he does run them. Just say what each one does before he runs it.
- Translate system concepts with a concrete analogy rather than the API name; drop internal identifiers unless he needs to type them.
- Don't flatten the reasoning. He asks "왜?" and follows real trade-offs — the fix is vocabulary, not depth.
- Tables and short sections work well; he reads them and acts on them.

This is about register, not capability. See [[assistant-name]] for what he calls me.
